# Electric-Shuttle-
An extended range electric shuttle with six seater layout. Displayed at the 2k25 anthapragnya and won the best prototype for mechanical engineering.
The principal aim of the shuttle is to bridge the gap in commutation in IIIT Basar campus
The cosmetic modelling of the shuttle is done in autodesk maya
mechanical modelling in fusion 360
mechanical simulation in ansys
electrical design on tinkercad
